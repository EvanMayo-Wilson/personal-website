I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Epidemiology at the UNC Gillings School of Global Public Health.

My research focuses on the benefits and harms of health interventions, artificial intelligence to assess and improve clinical trials and systematic reviews, and reporting standards that increase research transparency and openness. I am a principal investigator on two R01s, from the National Library of Medicine (NLM) and the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ), that use artificial intelligence to extract and compare the methods and results of intervention research. I also serve as Co-Scientific Director for peer review of Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Institute (PCORI) Final Research Reports. I am a member of the Cochrane Methods Executive, Associate Convenor of the Cochrane Adverse Events Methods Group, and member of the American Psychological Association Open Science and Methodology Expert Panel. I have authored multiple guidelines for reporting clinical trials and systematic reviews, and I am one of five members of the PRISMA Executive, which is responsible for maintaining guidelines for reporting systematic reviews. In 2025, Nature ranked PRISMA 2020, which I co-authored, among the 25 most-cited papers of the twenty-first century.
