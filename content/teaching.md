# Teaching

I advise PhD students in epidemiology. I welcome students interested in the program to [email me](mailto:evan.mayo-wilson@unc.edu).

I teach Systematic Review (EPID 702) in the Spring semester. In the Fall semester, I teach the Pharmacoepidemiology Seminar (EPID 893) and am a small group instructor for Epidemiologic Research Methods (EPID 726). I also teach at the [Summer Institute on Randomized Behavioral Clinical Trials](https://web.cvent.com/event/19906c19-dcf2-4bcf-812d-3531301d6f8d/summary) (sponsored by NIH).
